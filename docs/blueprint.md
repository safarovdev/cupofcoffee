# **App Name**: AromaFlow

## Core Features:

- Categorized Digital Menu: Displays menu items grouped by categories like coffee, tea, desserts, and breakfasts for easy navigation, echoing the visual layout of a traditional menu board.
- Detailed Product Cards: Each menu item is presented with a photo, a compelling description, a list of ingredients, potential allergens, and pricing information, inspired by the high-quality visuals of the source image.
- Customization Options: Allows guests to customize their orders by selecting various options such as drink volume (S/M/L), type of milk (oat, coconut), syrups, and other additions, enhancing the user experience.
- AI-Powered Dietary Companion: A generative AI tool that, based on selected menu items and specified dietary restrictions (e.g., allergies, vegan preferences), provides personalized ingredient explanations, substitute suggestions, or flag potential issues to the customer.

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary color is a vibrant, deep green (#1FB81F), symbolizing freshness and natural ingredients, serving as a lively highlight on the dark interface.
- A very dark, almost black background with a subtle green undertone (#161C16) provides a sophisticated and premium feel, mimicking the deep, rich tones of the inspirational image.
- The accent color is a bright, zesty yellow-green (#B3E62E), analogous to the primary color, used for interactive elements and call-to-actions to create a compelling contrast and guide user focus.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif font with high contrast, bringing an elegant and fashionable aesthetic to category titles and important headers.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif for descriptions, ingredient lists, and prices, ensuring readability and a modern, friendly touch that complements the serif headlines.
- Minimalist line-art icons are used for categories and customization options, inspired by the clean graphic elements in the original menu, maintaining a sleek and easily understandable visual language.
- A clear, grid-based layout for product display, ensuring quick scanning of categories and items, with prominent imagery and well-defined information blocks for each product.
- Subtle and fluid transition animations on category changes and product card interactions to enhance the user experience and create a smooth flow while browsing the menu.
